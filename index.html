Security Architecture Notice

Client-Side Processing: AUTOEDIT AI operates with no proprietary backend. All video rendering, frame extraction, and timeline operations run locally on your device.

Zero Full-Video Cloud Upload: For AI video analysis, only selected downsampled JPEG frames are transmitted over TLS to Google's official Gemini endpoint. Full video files remain on your device.

Model Flexibility: Supports Gemini 3.8 Flash, 2.5, 1.5, or any valid model identifier available in your Google Cloud / AI Studio project.
